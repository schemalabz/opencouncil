# <Country> (realm `<realm>`, <domain>, language `<lang>`)

Fill this before the first city of a new country, from research, with a link and the date checked for every source. Stop for review before importing. Then keep it current: each city that teaches something new updates this file, not the city notes.

## Bodies
Which bodies decide (council, executive committee, district councils) and which only prepare. Who presides each. Which other authorities (metropolis, county) to leave out.

## Sources, in order of trust
For each fact — current roster, functions and their dates, gender, groups/parties and their leaders, later changes — the official source (a national register, the installation minutes, the city's composition page, election results), what it misses, and how to filter it to one city.

## Roles (OpenCouncil mapping)
Each local function → city role, body role or party role; its title as the city writes it (rank, gender, delegation); `isHead`; which date it carries. Which functions are not imported, if any (Greece imports the General Secretary as a city role; decide per country).
Municipal entities (water utility, social or culture organisation, municipal company): does the country import the councillors who preside over them, and which public source names the current president? Greece: Diavgeia lists each entity and who signs its decisions (gr.md, step 11). «Not imported» is a valid answer; say why.

## Names
Order, case, particles, transliteration (Greece: ELOT 743), which source wins for spelling.

## Meetings
Where sittings and agendas are published, the video platform and how to list it, the kinds of sitting, how to find the start time.

## Boundary and logo
The official source of the municipality's outline (a national geography service; OpenStreetMap `admin_level` boundaries only as a fallback, with ODbL attribution) and how to filter it to one municipality. Where cities publish their logo and the usual terms for its use.

## Decisions
Is there a national decision portal? How decisions and minutes are published; how votes, attendance and proxies are recorded. Whether pollDecisions applies.
