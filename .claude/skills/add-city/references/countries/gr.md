# Greece (realm `greece`, opencouncil.gr, language `el`)

Term: 2024–2028 (elected October 2023). The second half-term started around 2026-07-01..07 (presidium and committee re-elected); prefer decisions from then on. Roster: a `factions` roster for `scripts/payload.ts`, which owns the Greek conventions (presidium, committee, factions); example `references/roster.example.json`.

## The city
Every Greek δήμος already exists in OpenCouncil as a `pending` city with its boundary. `list_cities` does not show pending cities: find the existing id on the site (`/admin/cities`) before `get_city`. Never `create_city` a Greek municipality.

The city's Diavgeia org uid (`City.diavgeiaUid`) is set by `scripts/import_diavgeia.ts`, and `get_city` does not show it. Put the org uid in the checkpoint report, so that an admin can check it on the city's admin page. Decision polling runs automatically only for a `supported` city, so the uid starts no work on a pending city.

## Bodies
- **Δημοτικό Συμβούλιο** (`council`): presided by its own presidium — President (mayor's faction), Vice President (largest minority), Secretary (second minority; with only two factions, the mayor's). The mayor is not a council member.
- **Δημοτική Επιτροπή** (`committee`): decides; chaired by the mayor or a deputy mayor the mayor designates; VP from the minority.
- Community councils (Δημοτικές/Τοπικές Κοινότητες): their presidents attend the council but are not members — not in the payload.

## Sources, in order of trust
1. **Diavgeia** decisions (council and committee minutes, mayoral acts). Read them as PDFs.
2. **The 2023 election**: `npx tsx .claude/skills/add-city/scripts/election.ts "<name>"` (public repo github.com/schemalabz/greek-municipal-elections) — seats per list and every candidate in vote order. Read its header: the winner's seats exclude the mayor, a minority list's seats include its head; candidates are in municipality-wide order, which is NOT who was seated where the municipality elects by electoral district. Nicknames are stripped, and many names have no accents («Τσουλλος», «Κωστας»). Use the lists for factions, never to decide who sits or how a name is spelled (see Names).

### Reading Diavgeia
Use the Diavgeia CLI (github.com/schemalabz/diavgeia-cli, v0.3.0 or newer) for every search and download, and the Diavgeia MCP for units and single lookups. The CLI splits a range longer than the API's 180 days by itself, and redirect the `--json` output to a file, so that a large answer never floods the session.
```
D="npx -y -p @schemalabs/diavgeia-cli@0.3.0 diavgeia"    # or: D="nix run github:schemalabz/diavgeia-cli/v0.3.0#diavgeia --"
$D orgs list --category MUNICIPALITY --json > <dir>/orgs.json   # the municipality's org uid (MCP: search_organizations)
$D orgs units <org> --json                                       # its units (MCP: get_organization_units)
```
- **Search by subject words**: `$D search query --org <org> --subject-words '<words>' --from <date> --to <date> --all --json > <dir>/<topic>.json`. The words are AND-matched, accents and case are ignored, and every word form matches («ορισμός αντιδημάρχων» finds «ορισμού Αντιδημάρχων» and «ΟΡΙΣΜΟΣ ΑΝΤΙΔΗΜΑΡΧΟΥ»). Each decision in the JSON list has `ada`, `subject`, `issueDate` (ms), `decisionTypeId` and `unitIds`.
- **Search the text of the PDFs**: `--content '<phrase>'` instead of `--subject-words`. The API searches the documents themselves, with the same word-form matching. It finds what a subject never says (an independent member, a resignation in a preamble), with no download. A single common word matches hundreds of decisions: use a phrase.
- **List one body's decisions**: `$D decisions list --org <org> --body "Δημοτικό Συμβούλιο" --from <date> --to <date> --json` (the committee: «Δημοτική Επιτροπή»). It resolves the body's unit and orders by protocol number. It fails when the body has no unit of its own. «Only inactive unit(s) match» means the body's own unit is inactive: pass the uid of the active unit that holds its decisions (often «ΥΠΟΣΤΗΡΙΞΗ ΔΗΜΟΤΙΚΟΥ ΣΥΜΒΟΥΛΙΟΥ»).
- **Mayoral acts** (deputy mayors, εντεταλμένοι, committee constitution, chair designation) are often all under the mayor's-office unit («Αποφάσεις Δημάρχου»): `--unit <that unit>` over the right months finds them together. Otherwise search by subject words («ορισμός αντιδημάρχων», «απόφαση δημάρχου»). Acts carry decision types `Α.2`, `2.4.7.1` **and `100`** (Διορισμός): filter by type only to drop pay orders (Β.2.2/Β.1.3), never to a single act type. Do not use `--query` (it matches any word, so pay orders flood the result), and `--content <name>` does not find a person.
- **Presidium, committee, committee VP**: subjects vary («Εκλογή Προεδρείου», «Εκλογή μελών νέου Προεδρείου», «Εκλογή Προέδρου, Αντιπροέδρου και Γραμματέα», «Πρακτικό … εκλογή Προέδρου»). Search both «εκλογή προεδρείου» and «εκλογή προέδρου» from June of the half-term year: Heraklion's 2026 minutes match only the second.
- **Read decisions as PDFs**: `$D decisions download <ADA> [<ADA> …] -o <dir>/pdf --skip-existing`, then have subagents read the pages with the Read tool (SKILL.md, "Read sources as page images"). Many municipalities publish PDFs with broken Greek fonts or scans; Chalkida prints a Latin «Y» inside a Greek surname («ΨΥΧΟΓYΙΟΥ»), which a text search never matches.
- A decision is sometimes issued weeks after its sitting and posted again as «ΟΡΘΗ ΕΠΑΝΑΛΗΨΗ» with different text: search past the sitting date, and the newest version wins.
- Record every finding with its ΑΔΑ in `sources.md`.

## The roster checklist (Greek specifics)
1. **Roster**: the newest full roll call (ΠΑΡΟΝΤΕΣ + ΑΠΟΝΤΕΣ = seat count). The July 2026 presidium election minutes («Εκλογή Προεδρείου») list everyone, but members change after July: confirm against the newest council minutes and their «αντικατάσταση/ορκωμοσία» items.
2. **Who took the seats**: compare that roll call with the first roll call of the term (the January 2024 presidium election). Every name on one and not the other is a replacement: search «παραίτηση», «αποδοχή παραίτησης», «διαπιστωτική πράξη», «ορκωμοσία», «επιλαχόντ», «κατάληψη έδρας», «πλήρωση κενής θέσης», «αντικατάσταση» near the date. Some cities publish no seat act: announcements in the minutes' preamble are evidence too, and the roll calls date the change (bisect them: the last one with the old name, the first with the new). A difference between the 2023 lists and the January 2024 roll call happened before the installation: do not search Diavgeia for it.
3. **Factions**: party per member from the 2023 lists. **Independents**: a member can leave a faction at any time in the term, and the change is often recorded only in passing — Heraklion's «αποχωρεί από την δημοτική παράταξη … και πλέον συνεχίζει τη θητεία της … ως ανεξάρτητη δημοτική σύμβουλος» (Feb 2024) appears only in a committee decision electing a new VP, and Alimos's only as «Σε περίπτωση ανεξαρτητοποίησης … η κ. Δασκαλάκη Νικολέττα δεν μπορεί να προταθεί» in the committee election minutes. Search the text of every decision of the term with three content searches: «ανεξάρτητος δημοτικός σύμβουλος», «ανεξαρτητοποίηση» and «ανεξαρτητοποιήθηκε» (`--content`, from the term start). Skip the hits that only quote the law (regulations, procurement), and read the others. Independents keep their list position (`"independent": true`). A member who leaves the majority is independent too. Order factions by seats. Inside a faction, the order is the 2023 municipality-wide votes, also where the seats were won by electoral district.
4. **Faction leaders**: the mayor leads the majority. For a minority, an explicit statement wins: «επικεφαλής της … παράταξης κ. X» in recent minutes, or a decision naming the leader (factions change leaders mid-term even while the 2023 head still sits). No statement ⇒ the 2023 head while seated. None ⇒ no Leader, and say so.
5. **Presidium** and its election date. A minority that names no candidate leaves the seat to the majority (legal). Check the newest council invitations/decisions for later changes (resignations, re-elections, a death): an officer who left gets an `endDate`; only the current holders and their dates go in the payload — earlier holders of the same office go in the report.
6. **Committee**: members and alternates in decision order («Ανάδειξη τακτικών και αναπληρωματικών μελών», «Συγκρότηση Δημοτικής Επιτροπής»); a later lottery («κλήρωση») can reorder alternates. The chair: the mayor, or a deputy mayor the mayor designates. Sources in this order: the mayor's «Συγκρότηση Δημοτικής Επιτροπής» act, the committee-VP election decision (its chair presides), then the invitations («Ο Πρόεδρος της Δημοτικής Επιτροπής»); the deputy-mayor decision only names a designation sometimes. A designated chair's start date is the designation act's date (a later re-designation replaces it). The VP is elected at the first committee meeting, from the minority (from the majority when the minority declines) — its own date. No VP decision on Diavgeia ⇒ read the roll calls of the first committee decisions after the συγκρότηση («(Αντιπρόεδρος)»). When a mayoral decision and a council decision disagree on the members, the newer one and the current invitations win; say so. **Changes after the election**: a member who leaves the faction cannot stay on the committee (Law 5314/2026 art. 116 par. 4), so every independent who left after the committee election means a committee change, and so does a resignation. Search «Αντικατάσταση … μέλους της Δημοτικής Επιτροπής» (mayor's act) after the election. The member who left keeps the place with an `endDate`. The alternate who takes the seat is listed twice: the alternate entry ends that day, and a regular entry starts that day (each list entry may be `{"name", "startDate", "endDate"}`). Rhodes, 2026-08-05: Κορδίνας left, alternate Σπανός-Παπαγιάννης resigned, and Κιαχαγιά-Μαγιόγλου took the seat (6ΝΕΧΩ1Ρ-ΟΓΚ).
7. **Deputy mayors** («Ορισμός Αντιδημάρχων», types Α.2/2.4.7.1/100; the newest decision or «ΟΡΘΗ ΕΠΑΝΑΛΗΨΗ» wins): portfolio, κατά τόπον units, start date. Write a short title per portfolio from the listed duties. Paid/unpaid («χωρίς αντιμισθία») goes in `sources.md` and the report, not in the title. Two city roles for one person (portfolio + κατά τόπον unit) are normal. Look for amending acts after the main decision («Τροποποιούμε και Συμπληρώνουμε»): they add people and portfolios. Check the list against «Αντιδήμαρχος κ. X» in recent minutes — a later list can omit someone who still acts. A deputy mayor who left: the next deputy-mayor act cites the resignation and its date in its «Έχοντας υπόψη». In July 2026 many cities restated all their deputy-mayor acts for Law 5314 (Thermi 14-07 and 02-09, every term cut to 31-12-2026): the newest restating act is the full list; keep each person's start from the act that first gave the current portfolio. Reading the chain of acts is a good subagent task: ask for the holder of each office today, with the act and date per change.
8. **Special-duty councillors** (εντεταλμένοι): often in the same decision, otherwise subject words «εποπτείας», «συντονισμού», «ανάθεση … σε μέλη του Δημοτικού Συμβουλίου» (the word «εντεταλμένων» often is not in the subject). City roles: «Εντεταλμένος/η Σύμβουλος <τομέας>» / «Councillor in Charge of <area>». Law 5314/2026 (from 29-06-2026) dropped the institution and Law 5321/2026 (art. 53, new art. 183) restored it: Thermi moved the areas to deputy mayors on 14-07-2026 and reappointed the same councillors on 02-09-2026. Use the date of the reappointment act, and say so.
9. **General Secretary**: «Γενικού Γραμματέα» (appointment «Πρόσληψη»). The attendance lines of council minutes («παρευρέθηκε ο Γενικός Γραμματέας κ. X») are the best evidence that he is in office; appointments can hide the name behind a registry number and pay orders can be voided («ΑΚΥΡΟ»). Missing 2026 pay orders prove nothing. A mayoral act that lists «Δημάρχου, Γενικού Γραμματέα και Αντιδημάρχων» (phone lines, vehicles) also names him.
10. **Role dates** (Greek specifics of the shared policy): presidium/committee start = the election (the sitting date in the minutes, not the `issueDate`, which comes days later); deputy mayors = the decision's term start; do not copy «έως 31.12.2026» as an end date. The law forbids a deputy mayor on the presidium: when a presidium election makes a deputy mayor an officer, look for evidence after the election that the person still acts as deputy mayor (pay orders, acts signed «Ο/Η Αντιδήμαρχος», the title in later minutes): evidence ⇒ keep both roles; none ⇒ end the deputy-mayor role at the election date. Flag it either way. A successor goes in once the council has declared him (διαπιστωτική πράξη / call to the oath) — flag a pending oath. The mayor's substitute (αναπληρωτής Δημάρχου) is a note in the report, not a role.
11. **Municipal entities** (ΔΕΥΑ, ΝΠΔΔ such as the welfare or culture organisation, municipal companies, school committees, a port fund): their presidents are often councillors, and production carries these roles. Run `npx tsx .claude/skills/add-city/scripts/entities.ts <municipality org uid>`. It lists the entities whose Diavgeia supervisor is the municipality, and for each one the signers with a president's post and how many decisions each signed in the last 120 days. The register's "active" flag stays on for past presidents (Rhodes ΔΕΥΑΡ: five «Πρόεδρος Δ.Σ.»): the one who **SIGNS** recent decisions holds the post. An entity under another supervisor does not appear: Rhodes's port fund is under the Region. Find such entities in the council's «Ορισμός μελών … Διοικητικού Συμβουλίου» decisions and the subject words of the entity type («Λιμενικού Ταμείου»), then pass their uids with `--extra`. Record the president (and a VP who signs) as a city role only when the person is a council member or the mayor; other presidents go in the report. Start date: the council or mayoral act that appointed the board; none found ⇒ the signer's registration date, and say so.
12. **Diavgeia units of each body** (`diavgeiaUnitIds` on the roster's `council` and `committee`; `populate_city` saves them). Metadata only, no PDF. Find each body's unit in two ways: `$D decisions list --org <org> --body "Δημοτικό Συμβούλιο" --from <a month ago> --json`, which resolves a unit named after the body, and the `unitIds` of the decisions that the research already tied to the body (its minutes and roll calls). The second way also works when no unit carries the body's name (Trikala: council and committee both under 100042121 «ΤΜΗΜΑ ΔΙΟΙΚΗΤΙΚΗΣ ΥΠΟΣΤΗΡΙΞΗΣ», and `--body` fails). Three cases:
    - The body has its own unit: `["<unit>"]`. Check that its recent decisions are that body's (Rhodes: council 83734, committee 100086486).
    - Two bodies share one unit: try to separate them by signer. Collect the `signerIds` of each body's known decisions in the unit (`$D search query --org <org> --unit <unit> --from <a month ago> --all --json`; names from `$D orgs signers <org>`). A body is usually signed by its president, and sometimes by a substitute. When no signer signs for both bodies, each body gets one `"<unit>:<signer>"` entry per signer of its decisions (Athens separates its 7 communities under 84655 in this way). When a signer signs for both bodies, the signer cannot separate them: give neither body a unit, and say so in the report.
    - No unit holds the body's decisions, or the unit also holds most other acts of the municipality (Samothraki): leave the field out and say so in the report. Never give an org-wide unit.

## Names
The form in the newest decision, full first names (expand «Κων/νος»), no nicknames; when decisions disagree (one or two surnames, hyphen), the newest roll call wins. Roll calls are often in capitals without accents (Rhodes: «ΜΗΤΣΟΥ ΙΩΑΝΝΗΣ»), and the 2023 data often drops them too. Take each name's accents from a lower-case decision that names the person: committee election minutes, mayoral acts, the January 2024 presidium minutes. Rhodes production had «Μήτσου» for «Μητσού» and «Ζανετούλλης» for «Ζανεττούλλης». Ask the subagents to copy names letter by letter and to say when the source is in capitals; never add accents by guess. Hyphenate a double surname only when a source does. English: ELOT 743 (ου→ou, αυ/ευ→av/ev or af/ef, μπ→b at the start / mb inside, ντ→nt, γγ→ng, γκ→gk, χ→ch, ψ→ps, η/ι→i, υ→y). Party names: the form in the 2023 data, Title Case.

## Meetings
- Video search: `yt-dlp --no-update --flat-playlist --print "%(channel)s | %(channel_url)s | %(title)s" "ytsearch20:Δημοτικό Συμβούλιο <name>"`; keep only the municipality's channel or a local outlet that streams it. Stream titles can name the wrong body (committee sessions titled «Δημοτικό Συμβούλιο»): match each stream to an invitation by date and start time. Most cities stream only the council; check the titles and start times for committee sittings before you say a committee has no video. A «Ζωντανή αναμετάδοση» page with no recording yet ⇒ report the date of the next sitting. Note invitations that only mention τηλεδιάσκεψη (no public stream).
- Agenda: the official invitation (πρόσκληση). Search in this order, and stop at the first hit:
  1. Every decision of the sitting prints the invitation's protocol number and date («ύστερα από την αριθμ. 96.552/24-9-2026 πρόσκληση»): read them first, so you know what you look for.
  2. The site's own search box, in the built-in browser («συνεδρίαση δημοτικού συμβουλίου», «πρόσκληση»). A web search and guessed URLs rarely reach municipal pages, and Diavgeia almost never carries invitations.
  3. The news sections, not the council's page: many sites publish each invitation as a press release or an announcement («Δελτία Τύπου», «Ανακοινώσεις»). Heraklion: «Συνεδρίαση Δημοτικού Συμβουλίου DD-M-YYYY» under Δελτία Τύπου, with the files «prosklisi» (the invitation) and «themata» (the invitation with every report, ~9 MB). Upload the invitation, not the report bundle. The committee's invitation there is only the text of a press release («Συνεδριάζει … η Δημοτική Επιτροπή»), with no file: print the page to PDF (step 7).
  4. A listing that a script cannot read (links built by JavaScript, files listed under a heading with no link): open it in the built-in browser and read the links from the page.
  While there, note any invitation for a newer sitting: it says when the next video comes.
- The invitation can list items before the agenda («Προτάσσονται προς ενημέρωση και συζήτηση», «προ ημερησίας διατάξεως»), unnumbered, often requests from faction leaders. processAgenda has no field for them: check how it numbered them (it can append them after the last item) and correct the subjects before summarize. A request from a faction leader also names the leader.
- Other places for the agenda: the site's «Προσκλήσεις»/«Συνεδριάσεις» page (Αγία Παρασκευή: `/portal/enimerosi/synedriaseis/dimotikoy-symvouliou` and `/dimotikis-epitropis`, where the invitation is the HTML page itself: print it to PDF); a listing can skip a sitting (Αγία Παρασκευή posted the committee's 50th invitation and not the 51st), so a missing entry is not proof — check the posts around the invitation's date; WordPress: `/wp-json/wp/v2/search?search=πρόσκληση`, `/wp-json/wp/v2/posts?search=…`, `/wp-json/wp/v2/pages?search=…`; when the REST API is blocked (401) or a custom post type is used, `/?s=πρόσκληση&feed=rss2` or the archive page. Some sites are Liferay or static pages: follow links from the body's page. When the site is down, a local news site often reprints the invitation (say so; prefer the official PDF). An invitation that contradicts itself: prefer a corrected reissue («ΟΡΘΗ») and check against the stream's `release_timestamp`.
- «δια περιφοράς» (by circulation) sittings have no video: create no meeting. Two sessions on one day (e.g. a regular and an accountability session) are two meetings.
- Time zone Europe/Athens: offset +03:00 in summer, +02:00 in winter.

## Boundary and logo
Every Greek municipality's boundary is already in OpenCouncil (pre-loaded); check it on the city's admin page on the site (`get_city` does not return it), and do not import another. Logo: the municipality's own site; the same rule on terms of use applies.

## Decisions
Diavgeia is the national portal. `pollDecisions` links and reads the decisions, after summarize. This skill sets each body's Diavgeia units through the payload (step 12). It does not set the decision conventions: they are set on the body's admin page.
